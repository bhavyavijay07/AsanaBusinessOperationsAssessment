Triage and Triaging logic

In what sequence do you address these three bottlenecks, and what is your rationale for resource allocation?

When there are multiple issues or bottlenecks hit simultaneously, triaging logic is based on the financial risk involved, immediate operational exposure and the constraints involved.
Based on the given situation the Triage of issues will be as follows

Priority 1: The Data Integrity Breach

This will be taken as immediate action is required involving Financial and Vendor risk

Rationale: This is an active financial risk since double-payments can drain the cash flow and key suppliers threatening to halt services can trigger downstream supply chain stoppages. This requires immediate containment to protect company capital and vendor relationships.

Resource allocation: Finance Operations and IT engineering teams will be assigned to immediately freeze automated payment scripts and affected audit ledgers and execute hold/communications with the critical suppliers.

Priority 2: The Revenue Bottleneck

Rationale: Since 15% of the Q3 target is massive and has an immovable expiration time of 5:00Pm, the pipeline has to be cleared as this requires a fast focused intervention so that the revenue does not slip into the next quarter.

Rescue allocation: A senior revenue engineer can be allocated to immediately see through the issue and manually override the provisioning pipeline to get it moving from Pending to Approved.

Priority 3: The Vendor distribution

Rationale: Although 1,200 orders are impacted, the bottleneck sits with an external third-party logistics provider experiencing a system outage nationwide. Internal teams cannot code a fix for the vendor's infrastructure; our role is strictly crisis management, customer communication, and contingency planning while waiting for their restoration.

Resource Allocation: Customer Support and Logistics will be assigned to lead proactive communication, pause automated shipping triggers, and monitor the 3PL's recovery status.
