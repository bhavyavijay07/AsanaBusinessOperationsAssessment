While resolving the Revenue Bottleneck, you discover that the manual workaround to force-provision the enterprise account requires bypassing a mandatory 24-hour compliance and data-privacy audit. The Sales VP is pressing you to bypass the check to secure the quarter's numbers, claiming "we've never had a compliance issue with this tier of client before."
Your Task:
Write a brief (max 150 words) executive decision note to the VP of Sales and Head of Compliance explaining whether you chose to execute the manual override or hold the contract for standard audit compliance.
Propose a single, actionable modification to the current Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for contract provisioning that eliminates this friction between revenue recognition deadlines and compliance controls.
Executive Decision Note
To: VP of Sales, Head of Compliance
From: Business Operations
Date: Q3 Close, 4:15 PM
Subject: Decision on Enterprise Contract Provisioning Override
Decision: We are holding the contract for standard compliance review. While securing 15% of our quarterly target is critical, bypassing data-privacy and compliance controls introduces severe regulatory liability, potential legal exposure, and audit failure risks that far outweigh single-quarter booking targets. Pushing unverified enterprise data breaks our governance framework.
We will prioritize secure, compliant onboarding so the revenue recognizes safely in Q4, and I am partnering with Compliance immediately to streamline this review window for future late-quarter closes.

Proposed SOP Modification:
To eliminate this friction moving forward, we will institute a "Pre-Cleared Enterprise Tier" in the provisioning SOP. For enterprise clients pre-qualified by Sales 10 days prior to quarter-end, compliance and data-privacy pre-audits will run asynchronously during contract negotiation, ensuring technical and regulatory clearance is secured before the deal reaches final signature.
