Business Operations case study - Technical assessment

It is 3:30 PM on the last day of the fiscal quarter. You are overseeing core business operations when three simultaneous cross-functional operational crises hit your desk:

The Revenue Bottleneck: The sales team just closed a major enterprise contract that represents 15% of the quarter’s target. However, the automated provisioning pipeline failed, leaving the contract stuck in "Pending." If the client isn’t onboarded and billed before 5:00 PM, the revenue cannot be recognized for Q3.

The Vendor Disruption: Your primary third-party logistics (3PL) provider informs you that a system outage has halted all outbound warehouse fulfillments nationwide. Operations are frozen, and downstream fulfillment SLAs for over 1,200 active customer orders are currently breaching.

The Data Integrity Breach: Finance alerts you that a recent workflow automation script corrupted vendor payment records, resulting in double-payments sent to select vendors and missed payments to key suppliers who are now threatening to pause services.
